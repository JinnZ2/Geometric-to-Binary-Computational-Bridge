# Reconstruction: Oral Technology as Instrument Specification

> **Confidence: split, deliberately.** Every physical mechanism cited here
> is documented and citable. The claim that a particular oral tradition was
> encoding *these* mechanisms is inference, and it is labelled as inference
> throughout. The value of the reconstruction is not that it is probably
> right — it is that it is **testable on a bench**, mostly with materials
> that cost nothing.

This page follows from [02-empirical-audit.md](02-empirical-audit.md),
Claim 5. That audit found that claims reading as mystical were, one changed
variable at a time, describing real instrumentation. This page assembles the
pieces into the instrument they appear to specify and lists the experiments
that would confirm or kill it.

---

## The central methodological point

The first-pass audit marked most of these claims FALSE and stopped. That
was a correct answer to the wrong question.

A procedure transmitted orally encodes **what to do**, not **why it
works**. When the "why" is reconstructed by someone outside the tradition —
or generated by a language model, which is where several of these framings
came from — the stated mechanism is a guess layered on top of a procedure.
Refuting the guess says nothing about the procedure.

So the audit needs two passes:

1. Is the stated mechanism right? (Usually no.)
2. Is there *any* mechanism under which the procedure works? (Often yes,
   and often standard physics.)

An audit that returns FALSE on pass 1 and stops has not finished. It has
also, in this case, discarded a working navigational instrument.

## The instrument: a field polarimeter

| Component | Role | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Calcite** (Iceland spar) | **Analyser.** Birefringent double-image nulling; locates the source under overcast and below the horizon | **DOCUMENTED.** Ropars et al. 2012, *Proc. R. Soc. A*. Accurate to a few degrees. This is the Viking sunstone and it works |
| **Quartz** | **Retarder / rotator.** Quartz is optically active — it rotates the plane of polarisation by a wavelength-dependent angle | **DOCUMENTED** optics. Role in the assembly is **INFERRED** |
| **Still water** | **Horizon + polarisation standard + differential baseline** (three jobs, below) | **DOCUMENTED** optics. Role **INFERRED** |
| **Home stone** | **Angular datum** — the emplaced parent crystal that fixes the reference direction | **INFERRED**, but mechanically forced (below) |
| **Stars** | Absolute bearing, independent cross-check | Trivially documented |
| **Haidinger's brush** | Unaided human perception of skylight polarisation via macular dichroism. Trainable; most people need to be shown once | **DOCUMENTED**. Means the instrument has a naked-eye fallback |

### Why the combination matters

This is the part that turns a curiosity into an instrument, and it is the
strongest single argument that the assembly is real rather than assembled
by hindsight:

- **Calcite alone gives you intensity matching.** You compare the
  brightness of two images and null them. The human eye is *poor* at
  absolute brightness matching.
- **Quartz in the path turns the null into a colour transition.** Because
  quartz's optical rotation is wavelength-dependent, the null stops being
  "these two patches are equally bright" and becomes "the colour flips
  here". The eye is *far* better at colour nulling than at brightness
  nulling.

That is the entire operating principle of a laboratory polarimeter. A
tradition that specifies both minerals together, rather than either alone,
is specifying the precision upgrade — and there is no reason for a
decorative tradition to converge on the one combination that fixes the
known weakness of the simple version.

### Still water does three jobs at once

All three are standard optics, and all three are needed:

1. **Artificial horizon.** A level reference. Required for any altitude
   measurement — no level, no latitude.
2. **Brewster reflection.** At ~53° from vertical, water reflects
   near-completely linearly polarised light. That is a **calibration
   standard of known polarisation**, free, available on site.
3. **Differential reading.** Compare direct sky against reflected sky.
   Differential nulling beats absolute intensity matching by a large
   factor.

In a glacial environment, unfrozen still water is seasonally scarce. A
tradition that flags it as a precondition for accuracy is tracking an
**instrument requirement**, not expressing a preference. That asymmetry —
a costly, inconvenient requirement stated as mandatory — is what
distinguishes a technical constraint from a ritual one.

## Three details that flipped on re-examination

### "The home stone must be in place" — from dead to load-bearing

First-pass reading: stone-to-stone signalling. Correctly rejected — there is
no channel.

Correct reading needs no transmission at all. Calcite cleaves on fixed
lattice planes, so **every fragment of one parent reproduces the parent's
crystallographic axes exactly**, relative to its own cleavage faces.
Therefore:

- Emplace the parent and survey its optic axis to a fixed direction.
- Every fragment is now a portable copy of that direction.
- If the parent moves, the reference frame is void and **all** fragments
  are invalidated simultaneously.

"Must be in place" becomes a datum-integrity requirement. This is the only
mechanism found in the whole cluster under which an emplaced stone is
genuinely *required* — no field, no carrier, no channel, just a surveyed
reference that everything else is calibrated against.

It is also, structurally, the same operation as
[`rebase.py`](rebase.py): a network of claims that all rest on one
surveyed datum, where moving the datum invalidates the base for everything
at once. NEG-11's validation gate is the same rule — you may not re-root
onto something unconfirmed.

### "Moon out is better" — from unexplained to confirming

Lunar skylight carries the same Rayleigh polarisation pattern as solar,
with the maximum-polarisation band 90° from the source.

At glacial latitudes in winter the sun sits at or below the horizon, which
puts the band near the zenith — useless for horizon work. The full moon
rides high, which puts the band **at the horizon**, exactly where the sight
is taken.

The moon is the better source there for geometric reasons. A detail that
looked like preference turns out to be forced by the observing geometry of
that specific latitude, which is a strong confirmation because it is
latitude-specific and could not have been guessed.

### "Sings back / hum felt in the hand" — from false to conditionally true

First-pass reading computed **compressional** modes for a chunky stone:
kHz–MHz, far above vocal range, dead.

Wrong modes. A thin, elongated or bladed crystal has **flexural** modes
scaling as t/L², which land at **200–800 Hz** — squarely in vocal range,
and felt directly when the stone is held.

**Prediction:** the singing stones were bladed or elongated, not chunks.
This is checkable against any surviving physical example (see T1).

Separately and at a different scale: a cairn or rock-stack cavity has
Helmholtz and structural modes in the vocal band, and some rock rings
audibly from internal stress state (ringing rocks, booming dunes, both
documented). The first pass reduced a described *stack* to a single crystal
and lost the cavity.

## The lost step order is recoverable

The account preserves the components but not the sequence. That is
normally fatal to a reconstruction. Here it is not, because **the ordering
is optically constrained**.

`source → retarder → analyser` is not interchangeable with
`source → analyser → retarder`; only one produces a usable colour null.
With three elements there are six orderings, and one works.

**Six trials on a bench settles it.** This is not an archaeology question
and it does not require access to the tradition, a site, or an informant.
It requires a piece of Iceland spar, a quartz plate, a dish of water, and
an afternoon.

## Open question: what the pitch was for

Two hypotheses, with a clean discriminator.

**H1 — pitch as data.** The song *is* the storage: bearings, distances and
landmarks encoded as melody. Hand heat is then just keeping the crystal
frost-free and thermally stable in a glacial environment — mundane,
necessary, unremarkable.
*Predicts:* pitch matters for the **song**, not for the **stone**.

**H2 — pitch as readout.** A natural uncut quartz element has a large
temperature coefficient of frequency; this is precisely why watchmakers use
specific cuts to zero it out. An uncut stone is therefore a **thermometer
whose readout is pitch**, and hand heat is the excitation and tuning input.

Why a navigator would want precise temperature: atmospheric refraction at
low altitude is ~35 arcmin and strongly temperature-dependent, and arctic
conditions produce extreme refraction anomalies — looming, mirage — that
wreck horizon sights. **A temperature reading is a refraction correction.**
*Predicts:* pitch matters for the **stone**, and only for low-altitude
sights.

Also under H2: hand heat shifts calcite birefringence measurably, so
warming the analyser is a fine-tune on the retardance. "The heat of the
hands mattered somehow" becomes a control input rather than a metaphor.

**Discriminator:** did the singer need accurate pitch to sing the *route*,
or to *match the stone's hum*? If the second, H2.

## The scent protocol

Spruce is a good candidate for the carrier, and it is mechanically
load-bearing rather than decorative:

- **Resin as fixative.** Spruce resin slows volatile release. The protocol
  requires a scent held at controlled strength over days with graded
  crossover, and a raw odorant cannot do that — it flashes off. A resin
  fixative is exactly the missing piece that makes duration-as-dose
  physically possible.
- **Bornyl acetate / α-pinene** are strong, persistent, and — critically —
  chemically *adjacent* to other conifer volatiles. The stated rule was
  that the replacement scent must be similar, never jarring. A conifer base
  supplies a family of near neighbours to step through.

So spruce is plausibly **both** the carrier and the substitution ladder.

The reconsolidation architecture this sits in: elevated arousal at
retrieval destabilises a trace (the precondition for rewriting), and graded
scent replacement writes into the open window. That is coherent and matches
the reconsolidation literature, which is well replicated.

**Safety bound, restated because it is independent of all of the above.**
Thujone is convulsant at dose; *Tanacetum vulgare* is hepatotoxic and
abortifacient. A corrected mechanism is not a licence to attempt the
protocol — a coherent-sounding rationale is exactly what would persuade
someone to try it. The arousal-manipulation hypothesis is testable without
a hepatotoxic plant; the plant is load-bearing only for the historical
reconstruction, never for the mechanism. See
[02-empirical-audit.md](02-empirical-audit.md).

## Discriminating tests

Ordered by how cheaply they settle something. T5 needs no access to the
tradition at all.

| ID | Test | Resolves |
|----|------|----------|
| **T1** | Is the carried stone **bladed/elongated** or **chunky**? | Bladed → flexural modes in vocal range → the hum is solved |
| **T2** | Does the clear stone **double an image**? | Yes → calcite → the whole polarimeter reconstruction stands |
| **T3** | Was accurate pitch needed for the **song**, or to **match the stone**? | H1 vs H2. H2 makes the hand-heat detail load-bearing |
| **T4** | Was the home stone **oriented deliberately** when emplaced — aligned to anything? | Yes → the angular-datum reading is confirmed and "must be in place" is fully explained |
| **T5** | **Six orderings** of calcite / quartz / water. Which produces a colour null? | The lost sequence. A bench test, not an archaeology question |

T1, T2 and T4 are questions about physical objects. T3 is a question for a
knowledge holder. T5 is an afternoon with three components.

**What would kill the reconstruction:** T2 returning "no double image"
removes the analyser and the polarimeter account collapses to nothing. T5
finding that no ordering produces a usable colour null removes the reason
for two minerals rather than one. Either outcome should be recorded here as
plainly as a confirmation would be.

## What this page is not

It is not a claim about what any particular people knew. It is a claim that
**a working instrument is consistent with the transmitted procedure**, that
the instrument's components are individually documented, and that five
specific observations would confirm or refute it.

The failure mode to guard against is the one this whole folder exists to
catch: a reconstruction that explains everything, is confirmed by nothing,
and grows a new epicycle each time a detail does not fit. Each row above is
attached to a test. Rows that fail their test get struck, not amended.
