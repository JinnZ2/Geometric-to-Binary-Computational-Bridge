## Numerical measurement versus interpretation

This establishes those numerical observations, not semantics, text-generation quality, universal rejection rates, bitrate, or neural-model transfer. Invalid-input fixtures show that the toy is not a hardened library.

Additive bookkeeping preserves `z = reconstruction + residual` for compatible finite coordinates, within floating-point error. Squared-norm defects expose cross terms. Neither establishes physical power conjugacy. There is no sensor/transducer or energetic port measurement in this folder. See the [semantic/physics audit](audit/semantic-physics-audit.md) for sources and the pseudothermal caveat to the existing glossary; that glossary is not silently changed here.
